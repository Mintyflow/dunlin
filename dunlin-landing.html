Six modules.
One operating system.

Every part of your workflow, from first enquiry to monthly reporting, in a single purpose-built platform.

01
📬
Enquiry & Lead Management

Capture every inbound lead in a visual pipeline. Automated follow-ups, AI-drafted responses, and full source tracking — so you never lose a prospect.

02
🏢
Space & Inventory

List and manage every space you offer — offices, hot desks, meeting rooms. Live availability, real-time occupancy, and flexible pricing rules.
