This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Dunlin collects, why we collect it, how we use it, and what rights you have over it. We have written it in plain English — if anything is unclear, please contact us at hello@getdunlin.com.

Dunlin is a UK SaaS product built and operated by Marvanova Ltd., a company registered in England and Wales. When this policy says "Dunlin", "we", "us", or "our", it means Marvanova Ltd.

1. Who we are

Data Controller: Marvanova Ltd.
Product: Dunlin (getdunlin.com)
Contact: hello@getdunlin.com

As the data controller, we are responsible for deciding how and why your personal data is processed. We comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO) at this stage of our growth, but we take data privacy seriously and you can always reach us at the contact address above.

2. Data we collect

We only collect data that is necessary to provide and improve the Dunlin service. Here is a clear breakdown of each category:

2.1 Account data

When you sign up for Dunlin, we collect:

  • Name — so we can address you properly
  • Email address — for authentication, product updates, and support
  • Company name — to personalise your experience and understand your context
  • Password (hashed) — stored securely via Supabase Auth; we never see your plain-text password

This data is stored in our Supabase-hosted database and is used solely to operate your account.

2.2 Billing data

When you subscribe to a paid plan, payments are handled entirely by Stripe, our payment processor. We do not store your card number, sort code, or any payment credentials on Dunlin's servers. We receive only non-sensitive billing metadata from Stripe, such as your subscription plan, billing status, and invoice history.

2.3 Apollo.io contact search data

Dunlin integrates with the Apollo.io API to enable contact searches. To use this feature, you supply your own Apollo.io API key. We use that key to make API requests on your behalf and display results within the Dunlin interface. We do not store Apollo contact data on our servers beyond the session needed to display search results.

Apollo.io contact data relates to third-party business professionals (your prospective leads), not to you personally. Your use of Apollo data via Dunlin is subject to Apollo.io's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

2.4 Companies House data

Dunlin queries the UK Companies House open data API to retrieve company registration information, registered addresses, and filing histories. This is publicly available data provided by HM Government under an open licence. It does not include private personal data in the typical sense — it relates to companies and their public filings.

2.5 HubSpot CRM data

If you choose to connect your HubSpot account, Dunlin will read and write contact and deal data to your HubSpot CRM on your behalf. This integration is entirely optional and only activated when you explicitly connect your account. We access only the data necessary to push leads and update records as directed by you. We do not use your HubSpot data for any purpose other than fulfilling the integration.

2.6 Landing page enquiry data

If you submit your email address via the contact or early-access form on our landing page (getdunlin.com), that form is handled by Netlify Forms. We receive your email address and any other information you voluntarily include in the form. We use this to respond to your enquiry and, with your permission, to send product updates.

2.7 Usage data

We collect basic usage data to understand how Dunlin is used and to improve the product. This includes:

  • Pages visited within the app
  • Features used (e.g. searches performed, exports made)
  • Browser type and general device information
  • Session duration and error logs

We do not currently use any third-party analytics platform (such as Google Analytics). Usage data is collected via our own application logs hosted on Supabase and Netlify infrastructure.

3. How we use your data

  • Providing the service — authenticating your account, running searches, displaying results, processing payments
  • Customer support — responding to queries and troubleshooting issues
  • Product improvement — understanding how features are used to make Dunlin better
  • Billing and invoicing — managing your subscription via Stripe
  • Legal compliance — keeping records we are legally obliged to retain (e.g. financial records)
  • Security — detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, or unauthorised access
  • Communications — sending transactional emails (e.g. receipts, password resets) and, where you have consented, product announcements

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for automated profiling or decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Active account data — retained for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it.
  • Billing records — retained for 7 years as required by UK HMRC tax regulations (Companies Act 2006).
  • Support correspondence — retained for 2 years after the last interaction, then deleted.
  • Usage and error logs — retained for up to 90 days on a rolling basis.
  • Netlify Forms submissions — retained for up to 12 months, after which we either delete or transfer to our own records and delete from Netlify.
  • Marketing consent records — retained for as long as your consent is active. If you withdraw consent, we will remove you from marketing lists within 5 business days.

7. Your rights under UK GDPR

UK GDPR · Data Protection Act 2018

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the following rights over your personal data. These rights are not absolute and may be subject to certain conditions, but we will always respond to your request within one month (or let you know if we need more time).

Right of access

You can request a copy of all the personal data we hold about you (a Subject Access Request, or SAR). We will provide this free of charge within one calendar month.

Right to rectification

If any data we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you have the right to have it corrected. You can update most account data yourself via your profile settings.

Right to erasure

Also known as the "right to be forgotten". You can request that we delete your personal data. We will do so promptly, subject to any legal retention obligations (e.g. billing records).

Right to portability

You can request your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g. JSON or CSV) so you can transfer it to another service.

Right to object

You can object to us processing your data on the basis of legitimate interests. You also have an unconditional right to opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Right to restrict processing

In certain circumstances (e.g. while a dispute is being resolved), you can ask us to pause processing your data without deleting it.

Right to withdraw consent

Where we process data on the basis of your consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing done before withdrawal.

Right to complain

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@getdunlin.com with the subject line "Data Rights Request". We will respond within one calendar month. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.

ICO contact: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF. Helpline: 0303 123 1113. Website: ico.org.uk

  • Essential cookies — required for the service to function (e.g. authentication session tokens). These cannot be turned off.
  • Analytics cookies — used to understand how people interact with Dunlin (only active where you have given consent).

For full details on the cookies we use, how long they last, and how to manage your preferences, please read our Cookie Policy.

9. International data transfers

Some of our third-party processors operate outside the UK (primarily in the United States). Where personal data is transferred to countries not deemed adequate by the UK Secretary of State, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place — specifically the use of UK Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or the UK Addendum to EU SCCs, as approved by the ICO.

In particular:

  • Supabase — data is hosted in the EU (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland), within the UK adequacy decision scope.
  • Stripe — operates under an approved transfer mechanism (SCCs).
  • Netlify — operates under SCCs for UK-to-US data flows.
  • Apollo.io — operates under SCCs. Note that your Apollo API key is provided by you; you accept Apollo's own terms when using their service.
  • HubSpot — operates under SCCs. This integration is optional and only active if you connect your HubSpot account.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately at hello@getdunlin.com and we will delete it promptly.
