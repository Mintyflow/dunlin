Traffic Light Email Verification
Green (VERIFIED) — safe to send. Amber (LIKELY / DNS OK) — warm approach recommended. Red (NO EMAIL) — use LinkedIn instead. Dual-layer check: Apollo + DNS/MX lookup. No more bounces.

Built-in Outreach Sequences
Pre-written, multi-step email cadences for lease renewal scenarios. Personalised with the contact's name, company, and location. One click opens your email client, ready to send. No copy-pasting, no forgetting to follow up.

Pipeline & CRM Sync
