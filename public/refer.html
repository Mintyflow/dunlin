The small print.

  • The referral offer is available to active Dunlin subscribers (paying, not in trial) only.
  • Your friend must be new to Dunlin — they must not have had a Dunlin account previously.
  • Your friend's £50 discount is applied automatically to their first paid invoice, after their 14-day free trial.
  • Your £50 credit is applied to your account within 48 hours of your friend's first successful payment.
  • Credit does not expire and can be applied against any future invoice. It is not redeemable for cash.
  • There is no limit on the number of friends you can refer — each successful referral earns you another £50.
