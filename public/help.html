What makes Dunlin different
⬆

Space Pressure Score™

A proprietary 0–100 urgency score calculated for every contact. It combines estimated lease urgency, headcount growth trends, recent funding activity, and decision-maker seniority. HOT (70+) means they likely need new space right now — not just that they work near an office.

📅

Renewal Radar Calendar

Using UK Companies House data cross-referenced with Apollo contact records, Dunlin estimates when each company's office lease expires. The Renewal calendar plots them across 30, 60, and 90-day horizons — your hit list, sorted by urgency, updated daily.

🎯

Traffic Light Email Verification

Two-layer confidence checking: Apollo's database confirms email deliverability, then our DNS/MX check verifies the mail server actually exists. Green = verified and safe to send. Amber = likely valid. Red = skip. No more wasted outreach on dead addresses.

🇬🇧

Purpose-Built for UK Flex Space

Not a US enterprise tool adapted for the UK market. Dunlin is built from the ground up using UK company data, UK-specific lease patterns, and UK decision-maker titles. At a price point that makes sense for independent flex operators, not just institutional landlords.

Step-by-step guide
1

Add your Apollo API key

Go to Settings → Apollo API Key. Paste your Apollo Organisation plan key. This unlocks live searches against Apollo's 270M+ contact database. Without it, you'll be in demo mode with sample data — all features still work so you can explore first.

2

Search by location

Enter any UK city, postcode, or area in the Search tab and click Find Contacts. Dunlin returns up to 25 Office Managers, Facilities Directors, Operations Directors, and similar decision-makers near that location — the people who control office decisions.

3

Review Space Pressure Scores

Each contact has a HOT / WARM / COLD badge with a 0–100 score. Start your outreach with HOT contacts (70+) — they have the strongest combination of signals pointing to an imminent space need. WARM (40–69) is your follow-up list. COLD (<40) is a longer-term target.

4

Check email confidence

Green (VERIFIED) = Apollo has confirmed deliverability. Safe to send. Amber (LIKELY / DNS OK) = likely valid, warm approach recommended. Red (NO EMAIL / INVALID) = skip for now or try to find via LinkedIn. Always work green first.

5

Open Renewal Radar

The Renewal tab plots all your leads on a calendar by estimated lease expiry. Filter by 30 / 60 / 90 days to build your weekly call list. Overdue means they may already be in negotiations — high urgency, high potential.

6

Enrol in an outreach sequence

Click into a lead in the Sequences tab and enrol them in the Lease Renewal Outreach (5-step) or Flex Operator Warm Pitch (3-step) sequence. Dunlin personalises each email and queues your daily tasks. Click to open your email client with the message pre-filled — ready to send in seconds.

7

Track in Pipeline and sync to HubSpot

Move contacts through New → Contacted → Interested → Converted in the Pipeline tab. Drag and drop. When you're ready, push your leads to HubSpot from the Settings tab — all key fields are mapped automatically.
