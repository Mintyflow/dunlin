This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies Dunlin (getdunlin.com) uses, and how you can control them. We have written it in plain English — if anything is unclear, please contact us at apps@marvanova.com.

Dunlin is operated by Marvanova Ltd., a company registered in England and Wales. This policy is designed to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR).

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit. They allow the site to remember information about your visit — such as whether you are logged in — so you do not have to re-enter details every time you return.

Cookies serve a range of purposes. Some are strictly necessary for a website or app to function at all; others are used to remember your preferences; and others help website owners understand how visitors interact with their site. There are also cookies used for advertising, though Dunlin does not use these.

Under PECR, we must tell you about any cookies we place on your device, explain their purpose, and — where they are not strictly necessary — obtain your consent before setting them.

2. Cookies Dunlin uses

We keep our use of cookies to the minimum needed to run a secure, functional service. Below we describe each category of cookie we use.

Essential / strictly necessary cookies

No consent required

These cookies are required for Dunlin to function. Without them you would not be able to log in, stay logged in, or use the product securely. Because they are strictly necessary, PECR does not require us to ask for your consent before setting them — but we still tell you about them here in the interest of transparency.

This category includes:

  • Authentication session tokens (set by Supabase) — keep you logged in during and between visits
  • CSRF protection token — prevents cross-site request forgery attacks, a standard security measure
  • Cookie consent preference — remembers that you have acknowledged our cookie notice so we do not show it repeatedly

Analytics cookies

Consent required

We do not currently run any analytics cookies. This section is a placeholder for if and when we introduce analytics in the future.

If we do add analytics, we intend to use a privacy-friendly tool such as Plausible Analytics or PostHog — both of which can be configured to avoid setting any cookies at all, or to collect only anonymised, aggregated data. We will update this policy and ask for your consent before any such cookies are set.

We do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics service that tracks individual users across the web. Any future analytics tool we adopt will be chosen with privacy as a primary criterion.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services that we rely on to deliver Dunlin. We do not control these cookies directly, but we have listed them below and linked to the relevant third-party policies for transparency.

Stripe

When you go through our payment or subscription flow, Stripe may set cookies on your device. These are used for fraud detection and session continuity during the payment process. They are essential to completing a transaction securely — without them, Stripe cannot process your payment. Stripe's cookies are described in Stripe's own Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Stripe cookies are only set when you interact with a payment form. They are not set on general browsing of the Dunlin product or marketing site.

Google Fonts

Dunlin loads fonts (Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans) from Google Fonts, a free font service operated by Google LLC. When your browser requests a font file from Google's servers, Google may receive your IP address and some browser information as part of the standard HTTP request. Google states that it does not use this information to track individual users or serve advertising.

Note: Google Fonts itself does not set cookies, but the font-loading request does transmit your IP address to Google's servers in the United States. If this concerns you, you can use a browser extension that blocks Google Fonts requests, or use a browser set to "Enhanced" or "Strict" tracking protection mode. This will not affect the core functionality of Dunlin — the app will fall back to system fonts. For more detail, see Google Fonts Privacy FAQ.

5. No advertising or tracking cookies

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or any cookies that track your browsing behaviour across other websites. Specifically:

  • We do not use Facebook Pixel or any Meta tracking tools
  • We do not use Google Ads or Google Analytics tracking cookies
  • We do not use LinkedIn Insight Tag or similar social network trackers
  • We do not share cookie data with data brokers or advertising networks
  • We do not engage in cross-site tracking of any kind

If this ever changes, we will update this policy, give you clear notice, and obtain your consent before any such cookies are set.

6. How to manage or disable cookies

You have several options for controlling cookies. Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent Dunlin from functioning correctly — you will not be able to log in or use the product.

Browser settings

All modern browsers allow you to view, manage, and delete cookies. Here are links to the relevant help pages for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Manage cookies in Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox: Manage cookies in Firefox
  • Apple Safari: Manage cookies in Safari
  • Microsoft Edge: Manage cookies in Edge

Further guidance

For comprehensive, browser-neutral guidance on managing cookies, we recommend visiting aboutcookies.org, an independent resource that explains how cookies work and how to control them across all major platforms and devices.

Opt-out of Stripe cookies

Stripe's fraud-prevention cookies are set during payment flows only. If you do not initiate a payment, these cookies will not be set. You can also refer to Stripe's Cookie Policy for information on managing Stripe-specific cookies.

Important: If you block or delete the sb-access-token and sb-refresh-token cookies, you will be logged out of Dunlin and will need to sign in again each time you visit.
